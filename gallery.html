Figure options

Optional fields you can set on any entry. All figures are generic stand-ins; anything you leave out is picked at random.

"figure": "woman" | "man" | "neutral",
"skin": 1-6,
"hair": "short" | "long" | "bald" | "bun" | "side" | "bob" | "curly" | "buzz"

Skin tones

Set skin to 1 (lightest) through 6 (deepest). Shown for each figure type.

Hairstyles

Set hair to override the style. Each figure type also has its own default pool.
